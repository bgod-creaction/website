Brazilian Roots

b.god is a Brazilian-born DJ who carries Forró not just as a musical genre, but as part of his cultural identity. Growing up with the rhythms of the Brazilian Northeast, he brings authentic knowledge of the music, the dance, and the community that surrounds it.

Forró — built around accordion, triangle, and zabumba — has grown into one of the most beloved partner dance scenes in Europe over the past decade. Whether at festivals, social dances, or cultural events, b.god delivers sets that honor the tradition while keeping the energy high.

Based in Germany — Available Across Europe

Currently based in Freiburg, Germany, b.god is available for Forró festivals and events throughout Germany, Switzerland, France, Austria, Netherlands, Belgium and other European countries. Festival organizers can rely on professional communication, punctuality, and a genuine understanding of what makes a Forró event work.
