What is D3D4?

D3D4 is the joint artistic project of b.god and it410, two DJs and close friends bringing Brazilian underground sounds into the European club scene. Together, they created D3D4 as a DJ duo and cultural platform focused on Brazilian Funk Bass. More than a performance, D3D4 represents their combined sound, energy, and identity — a growing cultural movement shaped equally by both artists.
