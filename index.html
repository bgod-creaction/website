About

Latino DJ aus Südamerika

b.god is a Brazilian-born DJ and engineer based in Freiburg, Germany — a Latino artist bringing authentic südamerikanische Musik to the European club scene.

His sound lives where Brazilian culture meets electronic music. From Baile Funk and Brazilian Bass to Latin House and Afro House, his sets deliver raw groove and percussive energy rooted in Südamerika.

Read more

Beyond electronic music, Forró also plays a special role in his artistic identity. Built around accordion, triangle, and zabumba, this traditional Brazilian partner dance has gained strong popularity across Europe in the last decade. Whether dancing or mixing, b.god carries this rhythm with authenticity and passion — a true brasilianischer DJ mit lateinamerikanischen Wurzeln.

Most of the time, b.god performs alongside his close friend and fellow DJ it410. Together, they form the artistic performance D3D4, a project focused primarily on Brazilian Funk Bass. D3D4 is not only a DJ performance, but also the identity behind a party series organized by the duo, representing their sound, energy, and cultural vision.

it410, a Brazilian musician, capoeira Contramestre, and DJ, was the one who introduced b.god to his first steps in DJing. Another strong influence in his journey is MaisUmDJ, a friend with whom b.god shares experiences, artistic growth, and the organization of events.

b.god is also a member of the artistic collective MANDABUSKA, which aims to create space for independent artists to produce, promote, and spread their art. Strongly connected to peripheral and immigrant communities, the collective actively supports and amplifies artistic expressions emerging from these realities.

Available for clubs, festivals, and private events in Freiburg, Baden-Württemberg, and across Germany. Latino DJ buchen — keep your dance floor moving.
