About

Identity

DJ b.god is a Brazilian-born DJ and producer based in Freiburg, Germany. He connects Brazilian club energy with underground European soundscapes, building high-impact sets for dance floors that want heat and precision.

Signature Sound

Expect a blend of Brazilian Funk, Bass House, Hardgroove, Forro, and Techno. The vibe is raw, fast, and rhythmic, made for late nights, peak sets, and unforgettable drop moments.

Based In

Freiburg, Germany. Available for club nights, cultural events, and private bookings across Germany and neighboring EU cities.
